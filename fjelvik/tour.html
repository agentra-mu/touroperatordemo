The trip in short

Seven days between the emerald lakes and pale limestone towers of the South Tyrol. You’ll row Lago di Braies before the day-trippers arrive, walk hut to hut beneath the Tre Cime, and eat dinners of speck, dumplings and mountain cheese in family-run rifugi.

Walking days run four to six hours on well-marked trails. Your bags travel by van between stays, so you only carry a day pack.

  • 6 nights in mountain huts and family hotels
  • All breakfasts and 5 dinners
  • Certified mountain leader throughout
  • Luggage transfers between stays
  • Venice airport pick-up and drop-off
  • Park and cable-car fees

Day by day

Transfer from Venice, a short evening walk above town and a welcome dinner with your guide.

Row the lake at dawn, then circle the shoreline trail and climb to the Malga Foresta meadow. 11 km, 420 m ascent.

Open pastures with views to Monte Cristallo. Overnight in a mountain hut. 14 km, 650 m ascent.

The classic loop beneath the three peaks, with lunch at Rifugio Locatelli. 10 km, 500 m ascent.

A ridge walk to Lago di Sorapis, a free day for spa or via ferrata, and a morning transfer back to Venice.
